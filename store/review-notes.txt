This is a hardware emulator for the Commodore Amiga (1985), permitted under
App Review Guideline 4.7. It ships with no games and no Kickstart ROMs.

Amiga Kickstart ROMs are still under copyright, held by Cloanto, and cannot
legally be distributed with the app, so the user supplies their own, exactly
as with every other Amiga emulator. Cloanto sell them as Amiga Forever, and
anyone who owns an Amiga may already have one. On first launch the app
explains this and offers a scan that finds and installs whatever is provided.

To evaluate a working emulator, place a Kickstart ROM (for example
kick13.rom or kick31.rom) in the app's Documents folder via the Files app,
then use Paths > Scan. A zipped ROM set works as-is, with no unpacking.
Adding any .adf floppy image alongside it gives you something to boot.

Without a Kickstart the app intentionally shows setup guidance rather than a
black screen, so nothing here is a crash or a non-functional state.

The app has no accounts, no login, no server, and no network features, so no
demo account is needed. It collects no data at all; the privacy policy is at
https://www.crownparkcomputing.com/privacy

The Music tab is a player for tracker modules the user imports. It ships with
none, so it is empty on a clean install; that is expected, not a fault.
